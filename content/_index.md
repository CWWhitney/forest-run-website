Forest Run is a wood-fired sauna and an icosahedron house on Emery Way in Camden, Maine, beside the Camden Snow Bowl. Cory Whitney bought the land in 2019, and in 2024 he and his father, Joel, began building.

It has been slow. Joel has been treated for cancer, and Cory was hurt in a cycling accident in Germany. We build as we recover.

![Timber frame of the house, seen from inside](/images/house/house-frame-interior.jpg)

## Plan

The sauna is finished and the house is nearly finished. The interior and the landscaping will be done this winter.

In January 2027 Forest Run opens as a place to relax. Forest Run Collective LLC will run it and rent the sauna by the day.
